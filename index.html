Seminar Project on Quasiconformal Mapping
Under Prof. S. Ponnusamy, IIT Madras
  • A guided reading and discussion seminar on higher-dimensional quasiconformal mappings.
  • Aims to extend the classical theory of conformal and quasiconformal maps originally developed for the complex plane — to ℝⁿ for n ≥ 3, a setting where the tools of complex analysis no longer apply and geometric methods take their place.
  • Began with the foundational language of isometries of ℝⁿ, building toward the analytic and geometric characterizations of quasiconformality covered in later chapters.
  • Maintaining a set of LaTeX notes alongside the reading, including a dedicated section compiling worked examples of isometries, as a reference for the distortion estimates the later material depends on.
  • Progress so far spans the first three chapters in full, with work currently underway on Chapter 4.
