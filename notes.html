Notes

Notes from an ongoing seminar project on higher-dimensional quasiconformal mappings, under the supervision of Prof. S. Ponnusamy at IIT Madras — following F. W. Gehring, G. J. Martin, and B. P. Palka's The Theory of Higher Dimensional Quasiconformal Mappings.

Topic 1
Isometries in Various Spaces

Definitions of isometries and linear isometries between metric and normed spaces, with eight worked examples — translations, rigid motions of $\mathbb{R}^n$, the shift on $\ell^p$, holomorphic isometries of $\mathbb{C}$, and stereographic projection with the chordal metric.

More details
Topic 2
Dilatation, Differentiability and Maximal/Minimal Stretching

Preliminaries on differentiability and Wirtinger derivatives, singular values and the stretching of a linear map, the linear/inner/outer dilatation and the comparison inequalities between them, and two fully worked examples verifying the maximal/minimal stretching theorem.

More details
Topic 3
Quasiconformal Mappings and Grötzsch's Problem

The definition of $K$-quasiconformal maps via the dilatation $D_f$, a full proof of Grötzsch's extremal rectangle problem via Hölder's inequality, three competing maps for the square-to-rectangle problem, and four further examples — global stretching, shear, and radial/logarithmic-twist maps — with visualized grid distortions.
